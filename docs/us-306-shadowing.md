## Implemented behavior

- Three original model responses covering Parts 1, 2 and 3. Band labels are learning targets, not examiner awards or computed scores.
- Exact transcript including punctuation, with measured word/sentence timestamps from the same generation run. WAV duration and all timestamp bounds are checked in tests.
- Continuous playback, Sentence-by-Sentence, Loop Sentence, sentence selection and 0.75/1/1.25 speeds. `AudioBufferSourceNode.start` schedules the selected segment duration, so sentence mode cannot play the following sentence until Continue/Space. Highlighting follows the Web Audio clock.
- Reusable recorder with explicit mic request, live waveform, elapsed timer, stop and temporary Blob callback. Capture is capped at three minutes/20 MB. Permission denial, missing/busy mic, incompatible browser and playback failures have guidance.
- Listen to My Voice, delete, re-record, download and sequential/simultaneous comparison. Starting recording stops model, personal playback and embedded video.
- Self-assessment and explicit completion confirmation. No Speaking band is generated by Shadowing.

## Persistence and privacy decision

The story requests temporary capture while the checklist requests saved audio reopening after reload:

1. New audio stays in RAM until the learner explicitly saves or downloads it. Navigating away/reloading discards an unsaved recording.
2. Optional **Lưu trên thiết bị** stores the Blob in IndexedDB under `[server-verified user ID, question ID]`. It reopens only for that account/question on the same browser origin. Re-recording is temporary until saved over the previous copy.
3. Success is reported only after the transaction commits. Failed/aborted writes do not replace an earlier saved recording and leave the temporary copy available.
4. Completion uses a unique account/question key and serialized transaction; concurrent confirmations count once. It persists locally, not in the team's cross-device dashboard.
5. No audio upload endpoint or Cloud Storage is used here. Upload failure handling is N/A; local-storage and session-verification failures are handled. Existing US-103 upload code from main is not used or changed.

This is browser-device storage, not encrypted cloud storage. It is not a security boundary against another person using the same browser profile/devtools or other same-origin scripts. Use separate profiles on shared devices. Identity is rechecked on focus and before save/completion. Blob URLs, tracks, timers, animation frames and audio contexts are released on replacement/unmount.

## Native-speaker content and YouTube

The interactive local models currently use **synthetic demo audio**, Microsoft Zira Desktop (en-US), explicitly labelled in the UI. It is not a recording of a human native speaker. Licensed human model recordings are still needed before claiming the native-speaker content requirement is complete.

Sources found at the user's request:

- [IELTS Advantage — Band 9 IELTS Speaking Sample Answer](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=12fKm7MubKc).
- [Fastrack IELTS — Speaking sample answers: Hometown](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=bUTMwkZN6n8).

These are click-to-load original YouTube embeds/external links. No video was downloaded/rehosted and no third-party full transcript republished. The local transcript and karaoke timings are not applied to these videos. Band claims in video titles belong to their publishers.

To supply licensed human media, replace the WAV, exact transcript and word/sentence alignment in `models.generated.json`, update the provenance label/data and verify every boundary. Never reuse demo timestamps for a different recording.

To regenerate demo media on Windows with Microsoft Zira installed:

```powershell
./scripts/generate-shadowing-audio.ps1
```

The generator uses 16 kHz mono/16-bit PCM to match the voice timing clock. Generated WAVs/JSON are included, so Windows voices are not needed to run the app or Linux CI.

The browser access host was unavailable during implementation. Manual acceptance remains:

- Play sentence 1 in Sentence-by-Sentence mode; confirm it stops before sentence 2. Continue/Space must be required to proceed.
- Loop a middle sentence at all three speeds and check audible word highlighting.
- Grant/deny mic access; speak and verify waveform/timer, stop, replay, delete and re-record. Verify errors preserve an earlier recording.
- Sign in, save and reload; switch accounts and confirm another account's saved recording is not shown. Verify storage errors are not reported as success.
- Compare sequentially/simultaneously; start recording during playback and verify other playback stops.
- Complete self-assessment and confirm once; reload and reconfirm without increasing the count.
- Check keyboard, desktop and mobile layouts.

Licensed native-speaker assets, real-browser/microphone QA, teammate review and shared integration remain before the entire story can be marked Done.
